After 7 months of piecemeal work, the new club website is ready for release! It's still not completely finished, but the structure and a big chunk of the content is pretty much is there. 

It's built on Astro, a static website framework known for being very fast. It's hosted on Cloudflare, with a domain provided for free by the incredible domain registrar Porkbun. 

We also use the content management system SveltiaCMS to allow for easier editing of our pages. It connects directly to git to make changes, and it's quite convenient not having to open an IDE to make page changes. 

![An image of the view of our content management system, SveltiaCMS](/uploads/posts/Screenshot%202026-09-27%20151946.png)
<p align="center">Blog post-ception?</p>

I created this website because most amateur radio websites out there look, well, outdated. While _I_ think their appearance as quaint and charming, it just isn't the modern standard anymore. Since our club's website is important for getting new youth into the hobby, having a website accessible for those who are not particularly patient or knowledgeable in ham radio is important. Our New Ham section is also a big focus of the website, since in my experience there isn't a super good web resource to describe getting into the hobby.  

However, I made sure to still add some nods to the websites of yesteryear with a guestbook, totally not stolen from QRZ. You can sign your name here if you are interested: [https://msjarc.atabook.org/](https://msjarc.atabook.org/)

You can look at our project here: [https://github.com/msjarc/msj_arc_main](https://github.com/msjarc/msj_arc_main)

Please feel free to send us feedback on our website, we'd love to improve!
