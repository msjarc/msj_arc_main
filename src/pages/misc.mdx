## Other tools

- [QRZ.com](https://www.qrz.com), call sign lookup and profiles
- [RepeaterBook](https://www.repeaterbook.com), find repeaters anywhere
- [HamStudy.org](https://hamstudy.org), study for your license
- [Grid Square Locator](https://www.levinecentral.com/ham/grid_square.php), find your Maidenhead grid
- [PSK Reporter](https://pskreporter.info/pskmap.html), see where your digital signals are heard
