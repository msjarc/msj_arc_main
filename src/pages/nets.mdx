A **net** (short for network) is a scheduled on-air meeting on a specific frequency. Nets are a friendly, structured way for new operators to practice getting on the air. A net control operator directs check-ins.

The schedule below shows the format only. Please confirm current frequencies and times with the sponsoring club before relying on them.

## Net schedule

| Net | Day | Time | Frequency / Repeater |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
