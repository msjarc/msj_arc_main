One of the best ways to grow in amateur radio is to connect with other clubs. Below are ways to find clubs near Fremont and across the country.

## Find a club near you

- [ARRL Club Finder](https://www.arrl.org/find-a-club) — search the national database of affiliated clubs by location
- [ARRL Sections](https://www.arrl.org/sections) — our area is part of the ARRL Pacific Division, East Bay Section

## National organizations

- [ARRL](https://www.arrl.org) — the national association for amateur radio in the U.S.
- [AMSAT](https://www.amsat.org) — amateur radio in space and satellite operation
- [TAPR](https://www.tapr.org) — amateur digital and experimental communications
