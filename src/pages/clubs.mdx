Clubs are a great way to meet amateur radio operators in your area and explore new topics. Usually, most clubs hold meetings once a month, with a new speaker every meeting to present on an interesting topic in amateur radio. There are many popular amateur radio clubs in the Bay Area, some of which are listed below, and we encourage all those who are interested to try attending a few meetings as a guest!

* [South Bay Amateur Radio Association](https://sbara.org/)\*
* [Palo Alto Amateur Radio Association](https://paara.org)\*
* [Mount Diablo Amateur Radio Club](https://www.mdarc.org/)
* [Oakland Radio Communication Association](https://www.ww6or.org/)
* Many, many more!

\*club officers are members of these clubs

## National & International organizations

- [ARRL](https://www.arrl.org) (National association for amateur radio in the U.S.)
- [LICW](https://longislandcwclub.org/) (Long Island CW Club, teaches morse code)
- [YOTA](https://youthontheair.org/) (Youth on the Air)
- [YACHT](https://yachtyouth.net/) (Young Amateurs Communications Ham Team)
- [AMSAT](https://www.amsat.org) (Radio Amateur Satellite Corporation)
- [IARU](https://www.iaru.org/) (International Amateur Radio Union)
