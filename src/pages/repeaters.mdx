A repeater receives and then retransmits your signal at higher power from a better location, dramatically extending how far you can be heard on VHF or UHF. To use one, program your radio with its output frequency, offset, and access tone (also known as PL tone or CTCSS tone). 

## Repeater list

Here are the two most notable repeaters from Fremont. N6NFI is particularly popular.

| Name | Output (MHz) | Offset | Tone (PL) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WA6PWW | 147.015 | +0.6 MHz | 103.5 Hz |
| N6NFI | 145.230 | -0.6 MHz | 100.0 Hz |

Offset just means the difference between the output and input frequencies of the repeater. So for N6NFI, you need to have your radio receive on 145.230 MHz and transmit on 145.230-0.600 = 144.630 MHz. 

We highly recommend using CHIRP to program repeaters into your HT.
