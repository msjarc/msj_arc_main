A **repeater** receives your signal and re-transmits it at higher power from a better location, dramatically extending how far you can be heard on VHF/UHF. To use one, program your radio with its output frequency, offset, and access tone (CTCSS/PL).

The table below shows the format only. Always verify a repeater's current frequency, offset, and tone on [RepeaterBook](https://www.repeaterbook.com) before use.

## Repeater list

| Location / Name | Output (MHz) | Offset | Tone (PL) | Band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example 2m repeater | 146.xxx | − 0.6 MHz | 100.0 Hz | 2m (VHF) |
| Example 70cm repeater | 44x.xxx | + 5.0 MHz | 100.0 Hz | 70cm (UHF) |

Standard offsets: 2m uses ±0.6 MHz, 70cm uses ±5.0 MHz. The access tone must match the repeater's requirement for it to hear you.
