## Club meetings

MSJ ARC meets during the school year at Mission San Jose High School. Meeting days, times, and room are announced to members and will be posted here. A live club calendar is on the way.

## Amateur radio calendars

Useful calendars for events, contests, and on-the-air activities beyond our club:

- [ARRL Contest Calendar](https://www.arrl.org/contest-calendar) — major operating events
- [WA7BNM Contest Calendar](https://www.contestcalendar.com/weeklycont.php) — weekly contest listings
- [ARRL Hamfests & Conventions](https://www.arrl.org/hamfests-and-conventions-calendar) — swap meets and gatherings
